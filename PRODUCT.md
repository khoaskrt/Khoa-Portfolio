# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and professional peers who are evaluating me for roles, collaboration, or mentorship conversations. They usually arrive with limited time and want a quick but credible understanding of who I am, what I have done, and how I think.

## Product Purpose
This portfolio exists to build personal brand trust and support career opportunities. It should communicate my story, achievements, work experience, education, values, and long-term vision in a way that feels strategic, professional, and human. Success means visitors can quickly decide to contact me, shortlist me for opportunities, or continue a deeper conversation.

## Brand Personality
- Business and operations minded
- Specialized in fintech industry context
- Management oriented and outcomes driven

The tone should feel clear, confident, and reflective, with storytelling that highlights decision-making, impact, and leadership growth.

## Anti-references
- Do not design this like a generic SaaS product or app landing page.
- Avoid feature-marketing patterns that feel corporate-template heavy.
- Prioritize personal storytelling over product-style conversion blocks.

## Design Principles
1. Story before spectacle: structure the page around narrative progression (context, challenges, actions, outcomes, direction).
2. Evidence over claims: pair each key message with concrete achievements, metrics, or examples.
3. Leadership signal clarity: emphasize decision quality, operational ownership, and cross-functional impact.
4. Recruiter-speed readability: make scanning effortless with strong hierarchy, concise copy, and obvious next steps.
5. Fintech credibility with human voice: balance strategic rigor with personal values and long-term vision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard navigability.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences for all non-essential animations.
- Keep language concise and globally understandable for diverse hiring audiences.
- Maintain readable typography and touch-friendly interactions across devices.
